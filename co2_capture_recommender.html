CO₂ Capture Recommender

Enter your gas conditions to get the best-fit capture technology and material. Rule-based screening, calibrated with a CCS project registry (Jan 2023, 417 projects).

Notes. The registry has no temperature, pressure or material data, so it supplies only the “projects using this technology on this route” prior (about 20% of the score). The remaining 80% is engineering screening rules (partial-pressure, temperature, scale, contaminant and purity windows) drawn from general literature values. Treat results as a first-pass screen, not a design; validate with process simulation or experimental isotherms.

Evidence status. Source gas presets (coal ~13%, natural gas ~4%, cement ~15%, syngas ~35% CO₂) and the membrane, sorbent and DAC figures come from a first literature pass. Most of those sources are not ScienceDirect open-access papers, and cost figures use different years and currencies, so they are shown as evidence, not used in the score.
